BACKGROUND:
- This is first-pass title and abstract screening for a systematic review.
- Maximize recall of potentially eligible randomized trials.

INCLUDE:
- Population: adults with the target cancer specified by the review.
- Intervention/comparator: the treatments specified by the review.
- Design: randomized controlled trial, or clearly described as randomized.

EXCLUDE only if certain:
- Animal study, case report, review, meta-analysis, editorial, conference abstract only.
- Clearly not the target cancer.
- Clearly not randomized.
